# R2 red proofs on the published 4.19.6 (tag v4.19.6, 964c1507), 2026-10-13

R2's test files were copied onto 4.19.6's code. The one new member they need, MaxRequestDocuments on
MongoDbTrackingOptions and on MongoTapOptions, was stubbed as a property that does nothing; nothing else was
changed. KRONIKOL_TEST_MONGO pointed at a mongod 7.0 replica set.

Every failure below is the fact's own assertion. Notes:
- The_shape_rule_has_a_version_a_run_records is the existing pin of the shape version, rewritten from 4 to 5.
- An_update_reply_offers_the_YAML_view_and_draws_its_counts_as_YAML (Playwright) clicks the second note's format
  button, the reply's since R2 gives the update a request note; on 4.19.6 there is no second note, so it fails on
  a 30 s wait for that button.
- Guards that pass on 4.19.6 by design: A_commitTransaction_has_no_request_note, LogFilterText_off_leaves_no_request_note,
  A_client_bulkWrite_over_two_namespaces_stays_against_admin, and in Playwright R1's find-reply and null facts.
