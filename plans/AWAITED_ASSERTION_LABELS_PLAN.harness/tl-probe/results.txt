Reading:
- The console logger (-tl:off, which a build with no terminal uses, as on CI) shows a high-importance or critical message
  at minimal verbosity and hides it at quiet.
- The terminal logger (-tl:on, the default in an interactive terminal since .NET 9) hides high-importance, coded and
  critical messages at minimal and normal verbosity, and shows them only at detailed. It shows warnings.
- A task's warning turns into an error under MSBuildTreatWarningsAsErrors or a WarningsAsErrors naming its code, not under
  TreatWarningsAsErrors alone. NoWarn and MSBuildWarningsAsMessages silence it.
